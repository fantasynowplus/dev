How to read it

Each game shows the market's price (what the sportsbook implies) next to the model's simulated result. On moneyline, the number to the right is how far the model moves off the market on the home side — an aqua ▲ means the model likes that side more than the book does; a red ▼ means it likes the other side. Spread and total bars show how often that side hit across all 20,000 sims. The Top Edge is the single biggest disagreement in the game.
